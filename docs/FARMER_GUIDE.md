# Farmer Guide

## Registration

Create an account at `/register` with your name, email, phone, and a
password meeting the strength requirements (10+ characters, upper case,
lower case, a digit). This creates a Farmer account — the only role the
public registration form can create.

## Submitting a support case

From your account (`/farmer/cases/new`), describe your crop, location
(district required, mandal recommended), and the issue you're seeing. Your
case starts in **New** status and moves through triage, assignment, and
resolution. You can see the full timeline of your own case, but private
staff/dealer notes are never shown to you.

## Dealer matching

If your case is assigned to a dealer, that dealer only sees the case
information necessary to help you — not your full profile. Matching is
based on your district/mandal against dealers who have opted in to
receiving farmer cases and service that area; a staff member can also
reassign your case manually at any time.

## Field visits

You can request a field visit from an open case. A field officer schedules
it (with automatic conflict checking against their existing schedule); you
can see the scheduled date/time under "My field visits."

## Notifications

You'll see in-app notifications for case assignment, responses, and status
changes. Email/SMS notifications only fire if your organization has
connected a real provider — this build never claims to send an email or SMS
it did not actually send.

## Privacy

Your farm profile stays private by default. A `public_data_opt_in` setting
exists for future public-facing features, but nothing about your profile is
shown publicly by this build's public pages.
