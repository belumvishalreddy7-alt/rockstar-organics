# Dealer Guide

## Becoming a dealer

Submit an application at `/dealer-programme`. Our sales team reviews
applications; you'll be contacted if more information is needed. On
approval, a dealer account is created automatically and you're given a
temporary password — sign in and you'll be required to change it
immediately.

## Your profile

From your dashboard (`/dealer`) you control:

- **Directory visibility** — whether your business appears in the public
  dealer directory.
- **Farmer case matching** — whether you receive matched farmer support
  cases.
- **Public phone/email visibility** — shown in the directory only if you
  opt in.
- **Service areas** — the districts/mandals you serve, used by the
  matching algorithm.

## Product availability

Declare availability (available/limited/unavailable/unknown) for published
products only. This never changes the official product catalogue — it's
your own declaration, timestamped, and visible to farmers searching the
directory by product.

## Farmer cases

When a case is assigned to you, you can respond through the case timeline.
Private notes you leave are never shown to the farmer. You only see cases
assigned to you, never other dealers' cases or unrelated farmer data.

## Enquiries

Submit bulk or business enquiries through the public enquiry channel; staff
track and respond to these through the staff dashboard.
